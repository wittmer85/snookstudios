# Word Hero support

Word Hero is a spelling practice app for iPhone and iPad. A child keeps word lists on the device, hears each word spoken, and studies the list or takes a quiz.

It needs iOS 17 or later.

## No account

Word Hero has no account and no sign-in. Lists are not tied to a Word Hero profile.

## On this device

Speech is on-device Kokoro. The spoken phrase is generated on the device. Words are not sent to a speech server.

Word lists stay on the device. Quiz history stays on the device. Deleting a word list does not delete quiz history. Study results are not saved.

The chosen voice and speaking speed stay on the device.

## Restore the remove-ads purchase

A one-time purchase is intended to remove ads. The product id is `com.spellingaid.app.removeads`. The intended price is $7.99. Family Sharing is intended to be on. This product is not in the App Store yet, so there is nothing to restore until it is offered.

When it is available:

1. Open Word Hero on the iPhone or iPad.
2. On the home screen, tap **Review**.
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
4. If Apple asks, sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Use that same Apple ID after the app is deleted and installed again. The App Store keeps the purchase. Word lists and quiz history stay on the device and are not part of the purchase.
